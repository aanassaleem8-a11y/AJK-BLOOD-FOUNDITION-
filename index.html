ABOUT OUR FOUNDATION

A community built around hope.

AJK Blood Foundation is a community-focused initiative created to connect blood donors with people who need blood during difficult moments.

Our vision is simple: make it easier to find compatible blood donors, support thalassemia children, and respond to emergency blood requirements through a trusted community network.
