ABOUT FOUNDATION

Blood Donation Is A Gift of Life.

AJK Blood Foundation is a community-focused initiative working to connect people who need blood with those willing to help.

Our purpose is simple: make blood support easier, faster and more accessible for families facing emergencies and for children living with thalassemia.

Emergency Support Helping families during urgent situations.
Donor Network Connecting donors with people in need.
Community Service Building a stronger culture of donation.
Thalassemia Support Supporting patients who need regular blood.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Clear. Purposeful.

Our donor information process is designed to keep availability records organized.

01

Donor Case Recorded

When a blood case is closed, the donor's name, blood group, area, phone number and donation date can be recorded for future availability tracking.

02

Recovery Period

After the case is closed, the donor remains unavailable in the public search during the required three-month period.

03

Available Again

Once three months have passed from the recorded case-close date, the donor can appear again in blood group and area searches.
